💡 Hospital Clinical Governance & Reporting Architecture

Executive Medical Governance

The Chief Medical Officer oversees all clinical quality, regulatory compliance, and patient care standards across the entire medical campus.

Division Leadership

Divisions separate medicine into Clinical Services (outpatient, diagnostics, cardiology) and Surgical Services (operating suites, orthopedics, neurosurgery).

Department Heads

Senior clinical consultants responsible for daily department triage, physician scheduling, specialized treatments, and clinical escalations.

Attending & Resident Physicians

Front-line medical practitioners conducting patient consultations, admitting patients, managing treatments, and staffing emergency rotas.
